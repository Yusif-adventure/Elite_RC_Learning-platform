Welcome to the Elite RC Learning Hub pilot. This guide explains exactly how to create your account, find courses, open lessons, save your progress, and get help if something goes wrong. Follow the steps in order the first time. After that, use the table of contents to find the section you need.

# 1. What you need before you start

1. A device with a web browser: a phone, tablet, or computer.
2. An internet connection.
3. A valid email address that you can open during sign-up.
4. A password with **at least 8 characters**. Write it down somewhere safe.

You do **not** need to install an app. The Learning Hub runs in your browser.

# 2. Create your account (first time only)

1. Open the pilot site: [elite-rc-learning.vercel.app](https://elite-rc-learning.vercel.app)
2. Click **Sign In** in the top-right corner.
   - On a phone, first tap the **menu icon** (three lines) in the top-right corner, then tap **Sign In**.
3. On the sign-in page, click **Create an account** below the **Sign in** button.
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Full name** - type your real name, for example Amina Bello.
   - **Email address** - type the email you can open now.
   - **Password** - type a password with at least 8 characters.
5. Click **Create account**.
6. Check your email inbox for a message from Elite RC.
   - If you see it, open it and click the confirmation link.
   - If you do not see it within 5 minutes, check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder.
   - You can also wait a few minutes and try **Sign in**; the site will tell you if your email still needs confirmation.
7. Return to the pilot site, click **Sign In**, and sign in with your email and password.

**Important:** Use one account per person. Do not share your password with anyone, including other members. Staff will never ask you for your password.

If you already have an account

1. Open [elite-rc-learning.vercel.app](https://elite-rc-learning.vercel.app).
2. Click **Sign In**.
3. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

After signing in, members go to the **Dashboard** inside **Student Space**. Staff accounts go to the **Staff Workspace**. If you are a member, you should never need the Staff Workspace.

# 3. Find your way around

When you are signed in, the left-hand menu (on a phone, tap the menu icon first) contains:

| **Menu item**       | **What it does**                                                                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard**       | Your home inside Student Space. Shows the course you are working on and your progress. |
| **Explore Courses** | The full course catalog.                                                               |
| **My Learning**     | Every course you have started, with your progress.                                     |
| **Profile**         | Change your name or password, and sign out.                                            |

At the top of the public site you will also see **Home**, **Explore Courses**, **About Us**, and **Sign Out**. On a phone, these are inside the menu.

# 4. Browse the course catalog

1. Click **Explore Courses** in the top menu or the left menu.
2. Use any of these to narrow the list:
   - **Search box** - type a course name, topic, or skill, for example drone or soldering.
   - **Category buttons** - click **All** or a category such as a workshop topic. Click **All** to see everything again.
   - **Difficulty menu** - choose **All levels**, **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced**.
3. The number above the list shows how many courses match.
4. Click a course card to open that course.

Categories with no courses yet show **Coming soon** and cannot be opened. This is normal during the pilot.

# 5. Open a course and choose a lesson

1. On the course page, read the description, **What you'll learn**, the instructor name, and **Course at a glance**.
2. Scroll to **Course Curriculum**. Lessons are grouped into sections.
3. Click a section heading to open it. The first section is open by default.
4. Inside the section, each lesson shows either **OPEN LESSON** or **COMPLETED**.
5. Click the exact lesson you want. It opens that lesson, not the first lesson in the course.

If you have already started the course, the main button on the course page says **Continue learning** and takes you to your next unfinished lesson. If you have finished every lesson, it says **Review course**.

# 6. Use a lesson

1. Read the lesson title and the **In this lesson** list.
2. Click **Start lesson**. The lesson resources appear.
3. Open any resource that is provided:
   - **Slides** - click **Open slides** to open or download the slide file.
   - **Short video preview** - click the play button on the video, or click **Open preview clip** if the video opens as a link.
   - **Full lesson video** - click **Watch the full video on YouTube**. This opens YouTube in a new tab.
4. When you are finished, use the buttons at the bottom of the page:
   - **Previous lesson** - go back one lesson.
   - **Back to course** - return to the course page (shown on the first lesson).
   - **Next lesson** - move on when the lesson is already marked complete.
   - **Complete & continue** - mark this lesson complete and open the next one.
   - **Complete course** - mark the final lesson complete and return to the course page.
   - **Finish course** - return to the course page when the final lesson is already complete.

**How completion works:** If the button says **Complete & continue** or **Complete course**, pressing it marks the lesson complete. If it says **Next lesson** or **Finish course**, that lesson is already complete. You can undo a completion at any time with **Mark as not done**.

**Sign-in reminder:** Progress is saved to your account only when you are signed in. If you see **Sign in to keep track of the lessons you complete**, click it and sign in; you will return to the same lesson afterwards.

# 7. Continue where you left off

From the Dashboard

1. Sign in and open **Dashboard** in Student Space.
2. The top card shows the course you worked on most recently, how many lessons you have completed, and the percentage complete.
3. Click **Continue learning**. It opens your next unfinished lesson.
4. If the course is finished, the card says **COMPLETED** and the button says **Review course**.

From My Learning

1. Open **My Learning**.
2. Under the started courses, find the course you want.
3. Click the course or its continue button to resume.

Courses you have not started are listed separately. Progress is based on lessons you marked complete. Renaming or replacing a lesson resets progress for that lesson.

# 8. Change your name or password

1. Open **Profile** in the left menu.
2. To change your name:
   - Edit the **Full name** field.
   - Click **Save name**.
   - Wait for **Your name has been saved.**
3. To change your password:
   - Enter your new password in **New password**.
   - Enter it again in **Confirm new password**.
   - Click **Change password**.
   - Wait for **Your password has been changed.**
4. Your email address is your sign-in and cannot be changed on this page. If you must change it, contact support.

**Password rules:** Use at least 8 characters and make sure both password fields match exactly. The site will show an error if they do not match.

# 9. Forgot your password

1. Open the sign-in page.
2. Click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the **Email address** you used to join.
4. Click **Send reset link**.
5. Check your email for the password reset message. Check **Spam** or **Junk** if it does not arrive within 5 minutes.
6. Open the link in the email. The site opens the **Choose a new password** page.
7. Enter your new password in **New password** and **Confirm password**.
8. Click **Update password**.
9. Wait for **Your password has been updated successfully.**
10. Click **Continue learning**, or sign in again with the new password.

If the button says **Send again in 30s**, wait for the countdown to finish before requesting another email. Repeated requests may be temporarily limited.

# 10. Sign out

1. Click **Sign Out** in the top menu.
   - On a phone, open the menu first.
   - You can also sign out from the **Profile** page.
2. Close the browser tab if you are using a shared device.

Always sign out on a shared or public device.

# 11. Troubleshooting

Try these steps in order. Stop as soon as the problem is fixed.

| **Problem**                                      | **What to do**                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The page says "Page not found"**               | The link may be incomplete. Open [elite-rc-learning.vercel.app](https://elite-rc-learning.vercel.app), then go to **Explore Courses** and open the course again. |
| **"Sign in" does not work**                      | Check that your email has no extra spaces and that CAPS LOCK is off. Type the password again. If it still fails, use **Forgot password?**                        |
| **You did not receive the confirmation email**   | Wait 5 minutes, check **Spam/Junk**, and confirm you typed the correct email. Then try signing in; the site will say if confirmation is still needed.            |
| **You did not receive the password reset email** | Check **Spam/Junk**, wait for the 30-second countdown, and try once more. If it still does not arrive, contact support.                                          |
| **You see "This area is for staff"**             | You are signed in with a member account. Members cannot open the Staff Workspace. Continue with **Explore Courses** or **Dashboard**.                            |
| **A video will not play**                        | Use **Watch the full video on YouTube** for the full lesson. For the short preview, try **Open preview clip** or another browser.                                |
| **Slides do not open**                           | Allow downloads and pop-ups for the site, or try another browser.                                                                                                |
| **Your progress disappeared**                    | Make sure you are signed in as the same member. Progress is saved per account. Refresh the page once, then reopen **My Learning**.                               |
| **A page looks broken or outdated**              | Refresh the page. If it stays broken, close the tab, open the site again, and sign in.                                                                           |
| **The site is slow**                             | Large slide and video files can take time on slow connections. Wait for the file to load, or switch to Wi-Fi.                                                    |
| **A button does nothing**                        | Refresh the page once. If it still does nothing, report it using the template in section 13.                                                                     |

If a message such as **"Your progress could not be saved"** appears, take a screenshot and report it. Do not assume the lesson was saved.

# 12. Pilot rules and limitations

1. This is a pilot release. Content, layout, and features may change without notice, and the site may be briefly unavailable while updates are deployed.
2. Course creation, editing, and deletion are restricted to staff. Members can read courses, open lessons, and save their own progress.
3. Some courses or categories may show **Coming soon** while content is being prepared.
4. Large slide files and video previews may load slowly on mobile data.
5. Do not share your password. Do not use another member's account.
6. Do not use the site to upload or share unlawful, harmful, or private content.
7. Your name, email address, and lesson progress are stored so you can sign in and continue learning. Contact support if you want your account removed.
8. Feedback is welcome. Use the issue report format below so the team can reproduce and fix problems quickly.
